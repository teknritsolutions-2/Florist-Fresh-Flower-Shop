About Bloom & Petal •

The Story Behind the Flowers.

A floral studio built around thoughtful design, seasonal beauty and meaningful moments.

OUR STORY

Flowers With Meaning.

Bloom & Petal began with a simple love of flowers and the belief that the most beautiful arrangements are the ones that feel deeply personal.

What started as a passion for seasonal blooms, natural textures and thoughtful composition grew into a floral studio devoted to creating flowers with feeling. Every arrangement is considered as more than something beautiful; it is a way to celebrate, comfort, connect and remember.

A studio built around flowers, people and meaningful moments.

OUR APPROACH

From Thought to Flower.

Every arrangement begins with a feeling and takes shape through considered design, seasonal flowers and thoughtful craftsmanship.

MEET THE FLORISTS

The People Behind the Petals.

A small team of floral designers brought together by a shared love of natural beauty, thoughtful composition and meaningful work.

Emily Carter

FOUNDER & LEAD FLORIST

Emily founded Bloom & Petal with a belief that flowers can make life's moments more meaningful. She is inspired by nature, changing seasons and the beauty in everyday details.

Sophie Morgan

FLORAL DESIGNER

Sophie finds beauty in natural textures, unexpected pairings and the quiet storytelling flowers can offer. She loves creating designs that feel effortless, organic and full of feeling.

Isla Bennett

FLORAL STYLIST

Isla is drawn to the movement, colour and emotion flowers bring. With a thoughtful and intuitive approach, she creates arrangements that feel personal, refined and full of life.

Different perspectives. A shared passion.
